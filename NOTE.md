# Retention and Advance on Invoices: implementation note

Module: `account_retention_advance` (Odoo 19, depends on `account` and `sale`). Tested on Odoo 19 Enterprise with the
Saudi chart of accounts (`l10n_sa`).

## Journal entries

Amounts are from the example in the brief (SAR company, 15% VAT, 10% retention, 20% advance recovery).

**1. Advance received.** A standard customer payment, ticked *Advance Payment* and optionally linked to the project.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bank / Outstanding Receipts | 200,000 | |
| 102011 Accounts Receivable (customer) | | 200,000 |

**2. Progress invoice** (one entry, generated by the invoice itself):

| Account | Debit | Credit | Due date |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 102011 Accounts Receivable: amount to pay now | 85,000 | | per payment terms |
| 102011 Accounts Receivable: advance recovery | 20,000 | | invoice date |
| 102016 Retention Receivable | 10,000 | | expected handover date |
| Sales | | 100,000 | |
| 201017 VAT Output | | 15,000 | |

When the invoice is posted, the 20,000 item is matched (partial reconciliation) with the advance payment. The advance balance
becomes 180,000, and the invoice's Amount Due, the payment registration and the aged receivable all show 85,000.

**3. Retention release** (*Release Retention* wizard, miscellaneous journal, one pair of lines per invoice):

| Account | Debit | Credit | Due date |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 102011 Accounts Receivable | 10,000 | | chosen in the wizard |
| 102016 Retention Receivable | | 10,000 | |

The credit is matched with the invoice's retention item. The debit is a normal receivable that the client pays like any
other amount due. A partial release releases only the amount entered, allocated oldest invoice first (editable per invoice).

**4. Credit note.** The credit note mirrors the invoice for the credited share: Cr AR (net), Cr AR (advance recovery
reversed), Cr Retention, Dr Sales, Dr VAT. The retention credit is matched with the invoice's retention item. The recovery
credit takes over part of the invoice's match with the advance, so that amount is available on the advance payment again.
Nothing that is already posted is changed: only new entries and (re)matching.

**5. Foreign currency.** All items are in the invoice currency. When items are matched at different rates (for example a
release in a later month), Odoo posts its standard exchange difference entry.

## Assumptions and design choices

- **The Retention Receivable account is a reconcilable Current Asset, not a "Receivable" type account.** Odoo 19 treats
  every receivable-type line on an invoice as amount due: in the invoice residual, payment registration, follow-ups and
  the aged receivable. That would contradict "the client owes 85,000 now". The retention line still carries the partner
  and a due date. On install, the module creates *Retention Receivable* next to the receivable account (102016 on the
  Saudi chart), and the accountant can pick another one in Settings.
- **Work done is the untaxed amount of the invoice lines.** Retention % and recovery % apply to it, and VAT stays on the
  full value. Sales down-payment lines are not work done.
- **Advance recovery is a second receivable installment** that is matched with the advance at posting. This keeps
  everything inside Odoo's standard reconciliation, so multi-currency and exchange differences work. The amount recovered
  can never exceed what is left on the advance (checked in the advance's currency, or in company currency when the
  currencies differ).
- **An advance is a posted customer payment with a journal entry.** If the payment method has no outstanding account,
  ticking *Advance Payment* before confirming forces one. Advances are not offered as "outstanding credits" on other
  invoices, so nobody applies the whole advance to one invoice by mistake.
- **Project means the sales order of the invoice lines.** If an invoice comes from several orders, the first one is used.
  Credit notes take the project of the invoice they reverse. Advances have a *Project* field, which is filled from the
  first invoice that recovers from them. Retention %, recovery % and the handover date can be set on the sales order and
  are copied to its invoices.
- **Credit notes reverse retention and recovery in proportion** to the untaxed amount credited, capped at what is still
  held or recovered. If part of the retention was already released, that part is credited on the receivable instead and
  matched with the released amount that is still open.
- **Reset to draft keeps Odoo 19's reconciliations.** The module does not re-create any line; on re-posting it only
  re-matches what is missing. If the amounts were changed, they are recomputed once. Retention can't go below what was
  already released or credited, and an invoice with released retention can't be cancelled (reverse the release first).
- **Lock dates.** Invoices and credit notes use Odoo's standard lock-date handling. The release wizard refuses a date in
  a locked period, and Odoo shifts exchange difference reversals after the lock date.
- **The report is as at a date** (accounting date ≤ that date), per company, customer and project, in company currency.
  "Total invoiced" is customer invoices less credit notes, VAT included.
  Released or credited retention is valued at the invoice's historical rate, and recovered advance at the advance's
  rate, so the balances tie to the open items. FX differences stay in Odoo's exchange-difference entries. The *Export to
  Excel* button produces an .xlsx file; the list and pivot views export too.
- **Printed invoice:** the deductions and "Amount to Pay Now" are added under the totals of the standard invoice and the
  Saudi bilingual tax invoice.
- **Multi-company:** the account, percentage and journal are set per company; advances, accounts and journals are checked
  against the invoice's company; the report has a company filter.

## Not done / limitations

- Advances recorded as Sales *down-payment invoices*, or only as bank statement lines without a payment record, are not
  handled. Neither is a payment confirmed without a journal entry and flagged as an advance afterwards.
- An advance matched manually outside the recovery flow is not counted as "advance recovered" in the report.
- Early-payment discount terms: the discount is applied to the amount to pay now. This has only been checked for posting.
- If the retention account is changed in Settings while invoices are still in draft, those invoices must be re-saved.
  Retention already booked stays on the old account.
- The partner ledger and aged receivable don't show retention (by design); use the module's report.
- No translation file. The Arabic labels on the bilingual invoice are only shown when the GCC dual-language option is on.
